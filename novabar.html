NovaBar

Live Activities & Contextual Status Overlays for Android

Stable v2.0

I built NovaBar because I wanted dynamic, glanceable status indicators on my Android phone similar to Samsung's Now Bar. By using stock accessibility and overlay configurations, NovaBar translates active background processes—like audio, watch timers, calls, or battery updates—into visual status bar pills without needing system root.

v2.0 Highlights

  • Completely Redesigned UI
  • Meet NovaGuy — Your Own Companion
  • Voice Recorder Live Activity
  • Multi-Activity Dashboard
  • Swipe Gestures
  • Refined Animations
  • New App Icon
